# HydroRecycle India — Research Edition Viva Guide

## 60-second opening
“My project is called HydroRecycle India. The physical model demonstrates a hydraulic waste-handling and compression concept using syringes. I then built a Python data-analysis system to study the problem mathematically. The system separates official source-based waste data from my experimental measurements and from educational simulations. It calculates collection efficiency, processing efficiency, compression efficiency and throughput, and it also applies statistics such as mean, standard deviation, ranking and Pearson correlation. In the hydraulic laboratory, I can enter my own measurements and calculate pressure, theoretical output force and compression efficiency. So the project is not only a model of a machine; it is a measurable engineering experiment supported by mathematics and data science.”

## Questions judges may ask
1. Why use Python? — It automates calculations, reduces arithmetic mistakes and makes the analysis reproducible.
2. What is your main formula? — Compression efficiency = (initial volume − final volume)/initial volume × 100.
3. What does Pearson r mean? — It measures linear association, not causation.
4. Why repeat trials? — Repetition lets us estimate variation and reliability.
5. Why distinguish simulated and official data? — Scientific integrity: assumptions must never be presented as observations.
6. What does Pascal's principle contribute? — Pressure applied to a confined fluid is transmitted through the fluid, enabling force multiplication with different piston areas.
7. Why might real force differ from ideal force? — Friction, leakage, deformation and imperfect alignment.
8. What is throughput? — Processed mass per unit time.
9. What is a limitation? — The prototype is educational and does not represent an industrial recycling plant.
10. What would you improve next? — Add sensors, repeated trials and uncertainty analysis.

## Demonstration order
1. Show the robotic arm.
2. Demonstrate the hydraulic movement.
3. Show compression concept.
4. Switch to dashboard.
5. Select a state.
6. Show collection and processing percentages.
7. Open Hydraulic Laboratory.
8. Enter one of your measured trials.
9. Explain pressure and force.
10. Finish with: “The machine demonstrates the engineering concept; the data system demonstrates how mathematics can evaluate and improve it.”
